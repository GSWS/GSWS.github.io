Welcome! I am a research scientist at [Thales](https://www.thalesgroup.com/en), specializing in **machine learning** and **computer vision**. My current work focuses on advancing next-generation fingerprint recognition systems — shifting from traditional computer vision techniques to deep learning–based solutions to significantly improve accuracy, speed, and user experience.

I earned my PhD in Computer Science from the [Boston University](https://www.bu.edu/) and my master degree in Electrical Engineering from [University of Southern California](https://www.usc.edu/).
