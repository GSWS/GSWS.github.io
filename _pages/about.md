Welcome! I am a research scientist at [Thales](https://www.thalesgroup.com/en) with extensive experience in research and development across **machine learning**, **computer vision**, **natural language processing**, and **multimodal learning**. Currently, my work focuses on next-generation fingerprint recognition systems — transitioning from traditional computer vision methods to deep learning–based approaches to significantly enhance accuracy, efficiency, and user experience.

I earned my PhD in Computer Science from the [Boston University](https://www.bu.edu/) and an MS in Electrical Engineering from [University of Southern California](https://www.usc.edu/). My academic research focused on multimodal learning, integrating pathology images with genomic data to predict cancer outcomes. I developed methods in weakly supervised and self-supervised learning to analyze large-scale gigapixel images. Additionally, I worked on optical character recognition (OCR), including both text detection and recognition tasks.
